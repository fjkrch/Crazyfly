Run `sha256sum data/connectome/raw/*.feather` to check the copies. The full graph's `body_pre`, `body_post`, and integer `weight` columns represent directed segment-to-segment connectivity and **raw connection counts**. They are not signed synaptic currents or physical conductances. The annotations table supplies curated neuron labels; the neurotransmitter table supplies aggregate **predictions**, including `consensus_nt`. A selected circuit's signed, scaled LIF weights are a separate model transformation recorded in its manifest and derived edge records. Selection, sign assignment, and LIF constants are modeling choices, not measurements supplied by these Feather tables.

The current `manifest.json`, `neurons.json`, `edges.json`, and `audit.json` represent a **derived 256-neuron leg VNC subset**, not the complete MaleCNS. It has 24 proprioceptive sensory inputs (four per ProLN/MesoLN/MetaLN and left/right root side), eight descending inputs, 200 T1–T3 interneurons, and 24 leg-motor readouts (four per nerve and soma side). Sensory, descending, and interneuron sources require `consensus_nt` ACh or GABA; motor-origin and self-loop edges are excluded. Cells are ranked by the documented connectivity scores, and an input without a selected path to a motor readout is replaced by the next ranked connected candidate in its group. The generated audit reports 5,103 directed edges, 3,549 assigned positive and 1,554 assigned negative, with input/output path coverage of 32/32 and 24/24, respectively. The full selection and tie rules are in the manifest and converter.

The derived edge records preserve `raw_synapse_count` separately from `weight`. The latter is the engineering mapping `sign × 0.5 × log1p(raw_synapse_count) / sum_pre log1p(count)` for each selected postsynaptic neuron, with ACh assigned `+1` and GABA `-1`. This is a dimensionless model weight, not biological conductance. The research loader requires the generated `manifest.json` with `status: "real"`, source release, exact extraction rule, stable input/output neuron IDs, duplicate-edge rule, weight mapping, neuron-model constants, relative `neurons_path` and `edges_path`, and SHA-256 checksums for both derived JSON files. Edges are directed `pre -> post`; the core computes with `W[post, pre]`. The small synthetic circuit under `tests/fixtures/synthetic_circuit` is for software tests and is rejected by research training.

The manifest sets a dimensionless LIF threshold of **0.047** and a surrogate-gradient beta of **1.0**. These were selected before the main comparison from short G1 encoder-current and training pilots. Threshold 1.0 produced no spikes; 0.05 became nearly motor-silent after one PPO update. Offline replay of that trace at 0.047 restored activity without motor saturation, and fresh two-update simulator pilots confirmed original and rewired motor activity. Beta affects the backward optimization derivative but not forward spikes; reducing it from 10 to 1 lowered the observed raw gradient norm from about 10¹⁴ to order 1 in one original-circuit pilot. The policy's PPO KL remained high, so training stability is unresolved. These are model calibrations on pilot traces, not measured fly parameters or learning results. The converter exposes `--lif-threshold` and `--surrogate-beta`; both settings are fixed across original and rewired circuit conditions.
